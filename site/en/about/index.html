About MIMA LABS

Creative coloring books designed by a small studio in Switzerland.

Why we exist

MIMA LABS creates coloring books with familiar subjects and easy-to-recognize illustrations. Our collections include farm animals and seasonal themes, with more activities planned for the future.

Human-reviewed creativity

We use creative tools, including generative AI, to explore and develop ideas. Every published activity is reviewed, selected and refined by a human for age fit, clarity and creative value.

What comes next

Coloring is the start. Future books may add drawing, missing details and simple imagination prompts.
