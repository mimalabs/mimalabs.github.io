# MIMA LABS V32

V32 simplifies the two areas that had accumulated too much UI chrome.

## Hero
- One static marketing message instead of two simultaneous carousels.
- One large book at a time.
- Only previous/next arrows + three dots.
- Swipe on touch devices.
- Amazon click behavior and Coming Soon logic preserved.

## Books
- Only cover, age, title and action/availability are shown.
- Descriptions, activity chips, counters and arrow controls removed.
- Roadmap teaser removed from the main product shelf.
- Landscape shows Farm, Halloween and Christmas together in three aligned columns.
- Portrait shows exactly one book per swipe with three small pagination dots.
- Full covers, hover shine and disabled Coming Soon behavior preserved.

All SEO, localization, analytics, GitHub Pages deployment, socials and legal routes remain unchanged.
